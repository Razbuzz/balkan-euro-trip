# Kit List, Maps & Emergency Contacts

## Daypack Gear (Essential for All Hikes)

### Navigation & Safety
- [ ] Local topographic maps (1:25,000 scale where available)
- [ ] Compass with map board
- [ ] GPS device (Garmin or similar) with waypoint marking
- [ ] Whistle (emergency signal)
- [ ] Waterproof matches or fire starter

### Hydration & Nutrition
- [ ] 2-3L water bottles/bladder (plan: 1.5L for short hikes, 2-3L for full day)
- [ ] Electrolyte tabs (Nuun, Zeroed tabs, etc.)
- [ ] High-energy snacks (bars, trail mix, dried fruit, nuts)

### Weather Protection
- [ ] Waterproof rain jacket (Gore-Tex or equivalent)
- [ ] Pack rain cover (or large trash bag as backup)
- [ ] Sun protection (hat, sunglasses, SPF 30+)
- [ ] Warm insulated jacket (especially for high altitudes)

### First Aid
- [ ] Personal first aid kit with:
  - Adhesive bandages (various sizes)
  - Gauze and medical tape
  - Antiseptic wipes
  - Ibuprofen/pain reliever
  - Anti-diarrheal medication
  - Antihistamine for allergic reactions
  - Moleskin (for blisters)
  - Blister treatment (2nd Skin, etc.)

## Hiking Footwear & Clothing

### Footwear
- [ ] Hiking boots with good ankle support
- [ ] Trail runners (lighter, for easier days)
- [ ] Camp shoes or sandals
- [ ] Hiking socks (merino wool recommended)

### Clothing System
- [ ] Base layer: Moisture-wicking shirts/pants (synthetic or merino)
- [ ] Mid layer: Fleece or insulated jacket
- [ ] Outer layer: Hardshell rain jacket and pants
- [ ] Hiking pants (convertible preferred)
- [ ] Hiking shorts
- [ ] Sun protection: Long-sleeve UPF shirt, hat
- [ ] Warm hat (for high altitude)
- [ ] Gloves (light fleece, for chilly mornings)

## Overnight & Multi-day Gear

### Sleeping System
- [ ] Light sleeping bag (comfortable to 5°C / 40°F minimum)
- [ ] Sleeping pad (closed-cell foam, lightweight)

### Shelter (if camping in Prokletije)

### Hygiene
- [ ] Biodegradable soap
- [ ] Toothbrush and toothpaste
- [ ] Quick-dry towel
- [ ] Wet wipes (travel size)
- [ ] Personal hygiene items

## Money & Currency

### Cash Requirements (Minimum per country)
- Bulgaria: **30-50 EUR equivalent** (small cafes, parking, markets)
- Serbia: **30-50 EUR equivalent** (vignette, small purchases)
- Montenegro: **30-50 EUR** (cash widely used, EUR official)
- Albania: **40-60 EUR** (most places cash only)
- North Macedonia: **30-50 EUR equivalent** (mostly card, some cash)

### Card Acceptance
- All major cities: Visa/Mastercard widely accepted
- Small towns: Cash often required
- Petrol stations: Card acceptance near 100% on main routes
- Mountain huts: Cash only

## Water & Food Safety

### Water Treatment
- [ ] SteriPen UV water purifier
- [ ] Chlorine dioxide drops (Aquamira)
- [ ] Microfiber towel for filtering
- [ ] Backup: Boiling water (minimum 1 minute, 3 minutes at altitude)

### Food Safety
- Eat cooked food in established restaurants
- Avoid raw vegetables unless peeled
- Drink bottled water in Albania (2-3 EUR)
- Tap water: Safe in Serbia, Montenegro, North Macedonia (boil in Albania)
- Street food: Generally safe in tourist areas
