## Day 1 — September 12

### Flight
W6 4428 · TLV 09:10 → SOF 12:00

### Drive
Drive to the town of Kruševac, Serbia. It's a bit of a dump, but there's a church worth seeing — see [TripAdvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g304083-Activities-Krusevac_Central_Serbia.html).

The drive itself is roughly 3.5 hours. The border crossing will likely be at Kalotina (Bulgaria) – Gradina (Serbia), the busiest crossing on this route. No vignette is required; there's a distance-based toll payable at toll stations. Israelis don't need a visa for visits up to 90 days.

**Note:** crossing on a weekend might take a very long time — it's a busy border between an EU and a non-EU country.

### Hike
None.

### Sleep
Plenty of accommodation in Kruševac on Booking.com, roughly ₪130/night. Also emailed Jastrebac Lake Resort (jastrebaclr@gmail.com) to ask whether camping is permitted and at what cost.

## Day 2 — September 13

From the city of Kruševac, about 20–25 minutes to the trailhead; from Jastrebac Lake Resort, about 5 minutes.

### Hike

### Drive
Drive to eastern Serbia — Vila Rada, Uvac (Unnamed Road, Družiniće, Serbia), around 4 hours.

### Sleep

There's also a campground at the trailhead ([map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/nJeeSWVb6n93N9bSA)), and a well-reviewed [restaurant](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PxzVaFScpGPJM1fo7) nearby: *"Slavica is amazing. Camping is free if you eat there. Showers and toilets are very clean."*

Boat tours available around the campsite, depending on arrival time.

## Day 3 — September 14

### Hike
[Vidikovac Molitva](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/serbia/zlatiborski-upravni-okrug/vidikovac-molitva?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 10.1 km, 483 m elevation gain (3.5–4 h), out and back. A beautiful spot above a snaking river, great for bird watching: *"Bird watching and beautiful views are highlights, with easy terrain. Challenges include poor trail markings and crowded viewpoints. Recommendations include starting early and parking near the trailhead."* [This spot](https://maps.app.goo.gl/cBYSL2bDA4W1FqH1A) looks like a good viewpoint for bird watching.

### Drive
Drive to Montenegro, to the Žabljak area near Bobotov Kuk — roughly 3.5 hours.

To cross into Montenegro, we need: a **Green Card** (proof of third-party liability insurance valid in Montenegro — most Serbian rental companies include this, but confirm before departing). The border crossing takes around 45 minutes. Montenegro does not use a vignette system.

**Visa:** Israeli nationals may enter, transit, and stay in Montenegro for up to 90 days with a valid travel document, without a visa.

### Sleep
**Note:** choose a hike for the next day first, then pick a sleeping option based on it.

## Day 4 — September 15

### Hike
Lots of hiking options around this area:
- [Bobotov Kuk](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/montenegro/savnik/bobotov-kuk?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 10.1 km, 892 m elevation gain, out and back. Situated within the UNESCO World Heritage Durmitor National Park, this is a challenging alpine hike to Montenegro's highest summit. The most common starting point is the Sedlo Pass trailhead.
- [Zminje jezero – Crno Jezero – Barno Jezero](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/montenegro/zabljak/zminje-jezero-crno-jezero-barno-jezero?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 10.6 km, 335 m elevation gain (3–3.5 h), loop. A wonderful loop past three tranquil glacial lakes within the UNESCO-listed Durmitor National Park.

**Note:** review all the trail options, choose a hike, and pick the previous night's camping spot based on it.

### Drive
Drive to around Eco Lux Prokletije (GQFP+PWM, Grebaje, Montenegro), 3.5–4 hours. 

### Sleep
Camping is available in [Grebaje Valley](https://www.google.com/maps/place//data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x135265de08b51523:0x45997171a96ff5d2?entry=gemini&utm_source=gemini&utm_campaign=gem-default) for €5, or [this lodge](https://www.booking.com/Share-aO1qm1) — nice, but a bit pricier.

## Day 5 — September 16

### Hike
- [Volušnica – Maja e Vajushës](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/montenegro/gusinje/volusnica-maja-e-vajushes?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 10.3 km, 978 m elevation gain (5.5–6 h), loop. Challenging but beautiful.
- [Valušnica](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/montenegro/gusinje/valusnica?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 7.6 km, 711 m elevation gain (4–4.5 h), out and back. A popular way to see the Karanfili peaks, part of the Dinaric Alps.
- [Volušnica Talijanka Popadija](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/montenegro/gusinje/volusnica-talijanka-popadija?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 8.2 km, 802 m elevation gain (4.5–5 h), loop. Stunning views, a gorgeous peak payoff, and great photo spots. Steep inclines and limited water sources are the main challenges — start early and go clockwise.

### Drive
Drive to Shkodër, Albania — roughly 2.5 hours; the border crossing should take around 45 minutes.

### Sleep
Lake Shkodra Resort — has camping and glamping options and looks pretty nice.

## Day 6 — September 17

Short research suggests Albania has fairly unremarkable hikes overall, with the best ones near the Montenegro border. Plan: a short, beautiful hike, then drive on to North Macedonia.

### Hike
- [Lake Shkodra Resort Extended Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/albania/shkoder/lake-shkodra-resort-extended-loop?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 7.6 km, 57 m elevation gain (1.5–2 h), loop. **Rest-day option** — probably best spent resting and enjoying a day by the river.
- Alternative: [Blue Eye Trail via Ndërlysaj](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/albania/shkoder/blue-eye-trail-via-nderlysaj?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 6.6 km, 416 m elevation gain (2.5–3 h), out and back. One of the essential hikes in the Theth region, ending at a gorgeous waterfall.

### Drive
The drive to Matka Canyon is fairly long — add a stop at the border crossing (Bllatë e Sipërme, Albania) to avoid routing through Kosovo. Total drive is about 6 hours, but since it's a rest day, we can split it up with interesting stops along the way.

## Day 7 — September 18

### Hike
[Dolna Matka – Saint Nicholas of Shishevo Monastery Loop](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/macedonia/skopje/dolna-matka-saint-nicholas-of-shishevo-monastery-loop?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 5 km, 362 m elevation gain (2–2.5 h), loop. Can be extended with the first 2.5 km of [Matka Canyon](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/macedonia/skopje/matka-canyon?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) (9.2 km, 235 m elevation gain, out and back, moderate).

The trail starts by climbing to the monastery, which sits in a phenomenal spot with a beautiful view, then continues to the dam, which looks amazing. From there, a 2 EUR, half-hour boat tour of the canyon is available. Not difficult, very beautiful — worth doing the full Vodno–Matka trail (about 4–5 hours) if time allows.

### Drive
About 3h45m, including a border crossing of roughly 45 minutes.

### Sleep
[7 Lakes Camping](https://7lakescamping.com/en/price-list/) — a 40-minute drive from the next day's hike; several Airbnb options are also nearby. Contacted [this Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/krilchev) to check about camping there.

## Day 8 — September 19

### Hike
[Seven Rila Lakes](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/trail/bulgaria/--9/seven-rila-lakes?u=m&sh=9xpqp8) — 8.4 km, 537 m elevation gain, loop, hard. Incredible views and well-marked trails; the main challenges are steep sections and crowds in peak season. Start early and stay hydrated.

### Drive
From the Seven Lakes to Sofia Airport is only about 2 hours — if we hike quickly, we could also spend an evening in the city.

### Sleep
Sleep is for the weak.
